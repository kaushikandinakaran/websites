What every restaurant site does

  • 1Order in one tapOrder and Book in the header; a sticky Order / Book / Call bar on phones.
  • 2A real, photo-led menuHTML text (not a PDF), search, filter chips, prices and photos.
  • 3Open now, at a glanceStatus, hours, address and directions near the top.
  • 4Short checkoutCart total always visible; pickup or delivery; no account; demo payment.
  • 5Book a tableThree fields and a confirmation, with clear error messages.

Everything here is a sample design. Business names, menus, prices, hours and wording are invented; address and phone are placeholders. Photos are stock photographs, not the client's food. Demo checkout only: no payment is taken and no order is sent.
