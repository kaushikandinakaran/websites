Flour & Fig, photo edition (sample design by Website Studio) - credits

All photographs and 3D scans are from Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 public domain (https://polyhaven.com/license; credit optional,
kept for the record). The panoramas are photographs of real places by Poly Haven photographers. They are used as
stand-in scenery for a sample design: they are not the client's café, and the site does not name or describe them.

360-degree photographs (scenery and lighting)
- pano `studio_country_hall`: Studio Country Hall, by Sergej Majboroda, https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_country_hall
  (stations 1-4: the café in morning light, the pastry, the cake, the pour)
- pano `bush_restaurant`: Bush Restaurant, by Greg Zaal, https://polyhaven.com/a/bush_restaurant
  (stations 5-6: the menu and the table by the windows)

Derived files in this folder (same CC0 licence as their sources):
- assets/pano/studio_country_hall_8k.jpg and bush_restaurant_8k.jpg (desktop), studio_country_hall_4k.jpg and
  bush_restaurant_3k.jpg (phones): resized from Poly Haven's full-resolution tonemapped panoramas and saved at JPEG
  quality 78. (bush_restaurant_6k.jpg and bush_restaurant_4k.jpg in the same folder are no longer requested.)
- assets/env-country-hall-pmrem.hdr, assets/env-bush-restaurant-pmrem.hdr (desktop) and the -lo.hdr versions (phones):
  each panorama's 1k HDR, downsampled to 512x256 (256x128 for -lo) and prefiltered with three.js PMREMGenerator (r160)
  into its CubeUV layout, saved as Radiance RGBE (rows in GL order). Baking once avoids compiling the PMREM shader
  in the visitor's browser.

3D scans (shared folder showcase/assets/polyhaven/models/, 1k versions)
- model `wooden_table_02`: Wooden Table 02, by Serhii Khromov, https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_table_02 (the oak table)
- model `croissant`: Croissant, by Greg Zaal, Dario Barresi, https://polyhaven.com/a/croissant (one on the board, three clones in the basket)
- model `carrot_cake`: Carrot Cake, by Greg Zaal, James Ray Cock, https://polyhaven.com/a/carrot_cake
- model `tea_set_01`: Tea Set 01, by James Ray Cock, Rico Cilliers, Jurita Burger, https://polyhaven.com/a/tea_set_01 (saucer, cup, teapot and lid)
- model `wooden_cutting_board`: Wooden Cutting Board, by Kuutti Siitonen, https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_cutting_board
- model `wicker_basket_01`: Wicker Basket 01, by Kuutti Siitonen, https://polyhaven.com/a/wicker_basket_01 (desktop only)
Phones, and the basket on desktops, load the scans without their normal and ARM maps.

Procedural (made in code for this design, no external source): the stoneware cake plate (lathe), tea surface (meniscus and ripples), pour stream and its splash ring,
steam, contact shadows, the film grade (vignette and grain), and the six fallback posters (assets/poster-1.svg ... poster-6.svg).

Fonts: none downloaded. System font stacks only (Palatino Linotype / Book Antiqua / Georgia for display,
Segoe UI / system-ui for text).

Libraries (shared, showcase/vendor/): three.js r160 and addons (MIT), GSAP + ScrollTrigger (GreenSock standard licence), Lenis (MIT).
