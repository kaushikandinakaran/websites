Derived files in this folder (same CC0 licence as the sources):
- assets/pano-sundowner-deck-6k.jpg, pano-venice-sunset-6k.jpg, pano-sky-on-fire-6k.jpg: the three panoramas
  resized from their 8k JPGs to 6144x3072 (JPEG q78) for desktops: keeps the first station near the 6 MB budget, the
  site under 20 MB, and each GPU upload shorter. Phones use the 4k JPGs from showcase/assets/polyhaven/pano/ directly,
  except the deck: assets/pano-sundowner-deck-4k.jpg.
- Retouch: in both sundowner_deck files (6k and 4k) a wall power socket and its cable on the central post are painted
  out with the post's own wood from just above (no content invented, only removed).
- assets/env-sundowner-deck-pmrem128.hdr (used on every tier; the 256 file stays in the folder unused) and
  assets/env-sundowner-deck-pmrem256.hdr: the
  `sundowner_deck` 1k HDR (downsampled to 512x256 first for the 128 file) prefiltered with three.js PMREMGenerator
  (r160) into its CubeUV layout and saved as Radiance RGBE, rows in GL order. Lights the props without a PMREM run in
  the visitor's browser.
- assets/env-venice-sunset-pmrem256.hdr and assets/env-venice-sunset-pmrem128.hdr: the same bake of the
  `venice_sunset` 1k HDR (carried over from the earlier Olive & Sea sample).
- assets/poster-deck.jpg, poster-tables.jpg, poster-water.jpg, poster-dusk.jpg: rectilinear still views cut from the
  4k panoramas above, used as the loading state and the no-WebGL fallback. poster-deck.jpg is cut from the retouched
  deck photo (no socket); poster-water.jpg looks over open water, away from the skyline (no recognisable landmark).

Changes made in code (not to the files): the table set's colour map is desaturated toward weathered grey on the deck
and slightly on the waterfront; citrus peel roughness is raised (waxy, not wet).
The `the_sky_is_on_fire` panorama has no props, so its HDR is not used.

FONTS
No font files. The page uses system fonts only (Palatino Linotype / Palatino / Book Antiqua / Georgia for headings,
Segoe UI / system-ui for text).

OTHER
- Built in code with Three.js (MIT, loaded from showcase/vendor/): the ground-projected photo sphere shader, the
  cross-dissolve, the film curve, grain and vignette pass, contact-shadow pools.
- No string lights or candles in this edition: they could not be made to sit believably inside the photographs.
- The page icon is an inline SVG data URI.
