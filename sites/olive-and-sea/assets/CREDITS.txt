Derived files in this folder (same CC0 licence as the source):
- assets/env-venice-sunset-pmrem256.hdr (desktop) and assets/env-venice-sunset-pmrem128.hdr (phones): the `venice_sunset`
  HDRI (1k; downsampled to 512x256 first for the 128 file), prefiltered with three.js PMREMGenerator (r160) into its
  CubeUV layout and saved as Radiance RGBE, rows in GL order. Baking once means the browser never runs the PMREM blur.
  The original 1k HDRI is no longer requested by the page.

Changes made in code (not to the files): the plaster texture is repainted lime-white at load, the painted chair is
tinted blue, the floor tiles are warmed toward terracotta.
