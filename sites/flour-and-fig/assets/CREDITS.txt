Procedural (made in code for this design, no external source): counter, wall and window frame geometry, plaster relief
shader, wall shelves with bowls, plates and crocks, cake stand and its lace doily,
crumbs, fig linen cloth (woven normal map drawn on a canvas), tea surface, pour stream, steam, light shafts, dust,
the out-of-focus garden behind the window, and the six fallback posters (assets/poster-1.svg ... poster-6.svg).

Fonts: none downloaded. System font stacks only (Palatino Linotype / Book Antiqua / Georgia for display,
Segoe UI / system-ui for text).

Libraries (shared, showcase/vendor/): three.js r160 and addons (MIT), GSAP + ScrollTrigger (GreenSock standard licence), Lenis (MIT).
