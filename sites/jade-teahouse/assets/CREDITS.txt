Derived file in this folder:
- assets/env-warm-restaurant-night-pmrem128.hdr: the `warm_restaurant_night` HDRI (1k), downsampled to 512x256 and prefiltered
  with three.js PMREMGenerator (r160) into its CubeUV layout, saved as Radiance RGBE. Rows are in GL order (bottom row first).
  Same CC0 licence as the source. Baking it once avoids compiling the PMREM blur shader in the visitor's browser.
