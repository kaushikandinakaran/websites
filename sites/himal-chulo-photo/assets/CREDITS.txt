Himal Chulo, photo edition (sample design by Website Studio) - asset credits

360-DEGREE PHOTOGRAPHS (Poly Haven, all CC0 1.0, https://polyhaven.com/license - free for commercial use, credit optional)
The photographs show real places taken by Poly Haven photographers. They are used as sample scenery only: they are
not the restaurant, and the site does not name or describe the real places.
The dusk photograph is shared from showcase/assets/polyhaven/pano/ (fetched with showcase/tools/fetch_polyhaven.py pano <id>):
desktop loads the 8k JPG, phones and GPUs without 8k texture support the 4k JPG.
The restaurant photograph is our own copy in assets/pano/ (warm_restaurant_night_6k.jpg for desktop, _4k.jpg for
phones), resized from Poly Haven's 8k JPG, with one change: a green emergency-exit sign above a door was painted out
by cloning the neighbouring brick course (so the photo does not read as one specific building).

- pano `fouriesburg_mountain_lookout`: Fouriesburg Mountain Lookout, by Dimitrios Savva, Jarod Guest,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/fouriesburg_mountain_lookout (the arrival at dusk)
- pano `warm_restaurant_night`: Warm Restaurant Night, by Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest,
  https://polyhaven.com/a/warm_restaurant_night (the room, the table, tea, menu and reserve stations)

LIGHTING (made from the same photographs)
- assets/env-fouriesburg-pmrem128.hdr: the 1k HDR of fouriesburg_mountain_lookout, prefiltered offline into three.js
  CubeUV layout (cube size 128) so the page skips the runtime PMREM shader.
- assets/env-warm-restaurant-night-pmrem128.hdr: the same for warm_restaurant_night (shared with sites/jade-teahouse).

3D MODELS (Poly Haven, CC0 1.0), shared from showcase/assets/polyhaven/models/, 1k resolution
- model `wooden_table_02`: Wooden Table 02, by Serhii Khromov, https://polyhaven.com/a/wooden_table_02
  (the dining table; desaturated, darkened and made matte in code to match the room's wood. `WoodenTable_02` from the brief is a 30 cm stool, so this
  dining-size table is used instead)

- model `food_lime_01`: Food Lime 01, by Oliver Harries, https://polyhaven.com/a/food_lime_01 (loaded without its normal map)

MADE IN CODE (original to this sample, Three.js r160, MIT, loaded from showcase/vendor/)
- Prayer-flag cloth (printed-cotton texture drawn on a canvas: abstract print marks, no real script or text) and a
  marigold garland, the only additions to the dusk photograph.
- Brass thali and katoris, rice, dal, greens, curry, pickle, bamboo momo steamer and pleated momos, tea stream,
  steam, the diya flame glow, contact shadows. Food is illustrative, not photographic.
- Film grain (SVG noise) and vignette in CSS.

FONTS

  Segoe UI / system-ui for text).

OTHER
