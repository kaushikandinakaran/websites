The spice shelf

Two Himalayan notes

Timur
A Himalayan pepper, a relative of Sichuan pepper, with a citrus aroma and a gentle tingle. It lifts the achar, the choila and the paneer tikka.
Jimbu
A dried Himalayan herb of the onion family. Fried briefly in ghee, it gives the dal a soft, savoury, garlic-chive note.
