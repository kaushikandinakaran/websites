Derived files in this folder (made from the Poly Haven files above, same CC0 licence):
- env-*-pmrem128.hdr: each panorama's 1k HDR prefiltered offline into the PMREM (CubeUV, 128) layout three.js uses
  for image-based lighting, so the page skips the runtime PMREM step.
- bar_6k.jpg / bar_4k.jpg: the warm_bar panorama retouched (cloned from neighbouring wood and brick, or dimmed): two
  drinks-brand bar mats, two TV screens over the bar, a brand sign, three screens seen through interior windows,
  a payment card with a QR code, a purple light spill, and the cold glow of the drinks fridges; desktop 6k, phones 4k.
- cellar_6k.jpg / cellar_3k.jpg, dinner_6k.jpg: re-sampled panoramas (desktop 6k, phones 3k / the 4k original).
- m/*_512.jpg: 512 px copies of the props' colour maps, used on phones only.

Not loaded on purpose: normal and ARM maps of the bottles and onion, the barrel ARM map, label-paper and glass
normal maps; phones also skip every normal and ARM map.

Made in code for this site (no third-party assets): the panorama shader (ground projection, grade, background softening, the gentle movement and
flicker applied to the photo's own fire pixels), contact shadows, vignette and film grain.
