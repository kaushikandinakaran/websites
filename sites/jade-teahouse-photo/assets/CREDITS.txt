Jade Teahouse, photo edition (sample design by Website Studio) - asset credits

All photographs and 3D assets: Poly Haven, CC0 1.0 (https://polyhaven.com/license). Free for commercial use, no attribution
required; listed for the record. The places in the photographs are real places photographed by Poly Haven photographers.
They are sample scenery only: they are not this restaurant, and the site does not name or describe them.

360-degree photographs (the scenery)
- pano `chinese_garden`: Chinese Garden, by Andreas Mischok, https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_garden
  (stations 1, 2 and 5: arrival, the tea table, reserve)
- hdri `warm_restaurant_night`: Warm Restaurant Night, by Greg Zaal, Jarod Guest, https://polyhaven.com/a/warm_restaurant_night
  (stations 3 and 4: the steamer on the photographed table, the menu)

Derived files in this folder (same CC0 licence as their sources):
- assets/pano/chinese_garden_6k.jpg (desktop) and _4k.jpg (phones): re-encoded from Poly Haven's 8k and 4k JPGs
  (6144x3072 at JPEG quality 78; 4096x2048 at quality 76). Retouched: the street seen through the moon gate is covered
  with the hedge above it (mirrored), so no public road shows.
- assets/pano/warm_restaurant_night_6k.jpg (desktop) and _4k.jpg (phones): re-encoded from the 8k and 4k JPGs
  (quality 80). Retouched: the emergency-exit sign above the doors is replaced by the neighbouring brick course.
- assets/poster-*.jpg: 800x500 crops of the two photographs at each station's view (WebGL-off fallback).
- assets/env-chinese-garden-pmrem128.hdr: the `chinese_garden` 1k HDR, box-downsampled to 512x256 and prefiltered with
  three.js PMREMGenerator (r160) into its CubeUV layout, saved as Radiance RGBE (run-length encoded). Rows in GL order.
- assets/env-warm-restaurant-night-pmrem128.hdr: the same treatment for `warm_restaurant_night` (carried over from the
  earlier Jade Teahouse site). Baking these once avoids compiling the PMREM blur shader in the visitor's browser.

3D models (files shared from showcase/assets/polyhaven/, 1k)
- model `chinese_tea_table`: Chinese Tea Table, by Kirill Sannikov, https://polyhaven.com/a/chinese_tea_table   (the garden tea table; the same model again under the steamer in the dining room)
- model `tea_set_01`: Tea Set 01, by James Ray Cock, Rico Cilliers, Jurita Burger, https://polyhaven.com/a/tea_set_01
  (teapot, lid, two cups, two saucers, plus a copy of one cup and saucer beside the steamer; a European-style porcelain set,

Procedural (made in code, no external source): bamboo steamer and dumplings (canvas-drawn band, weave and liner
textures), steam, tea liquid and stream, contact shadows.

Segoe UI / Helvetica Neue / system-ui for text).
