Philosophy

Rice first. Then fish. Then timing.

A short counter, a small menu, and a chef who would rather serve you one perfect piece than ten good ones.

  1. I

    Rice

    Seasoned with vinegar and served warm, so each piece holds together and falls apart at the right moment. [to confirm]

  2. II

    Fish

    Bought for the day and cut to order. When something is not right, it is not on the menu. [Sourcing: client to confirm]

  3. III

    Timing

    At the counter, a piece is placed in front of you and should be eaten within seconds. Takeaway is boxed as late as possible.
