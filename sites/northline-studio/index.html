01 — Practice

Our practice

We begin with the land, the light and the people who will use the building, then we remove everything that does not help. What is left should feel inevitable, calm and built to last.

03 — Process

A plan, drawn in four moves.

  1. 01

    Site

    We start with the boundary, the sun path and what is already there.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Walls and grid first. The skeleton decides how the rest can behave.

  3. 03

    Rooms

    Spaces are named and sized around daily routines, not around furniture.

  4. 04

    Openings

    Doors and windows come last: where we look out, and where we come in.
