01 The dive

Leave the noise at the surface

Light bends, sound softens, and the day slows down. This design is built around that feeling: calm, cool colours and a menu that lets the sea speak first.

Three ways to enjoy the water

  • Raw Shells served on ice.
  • Chilled Cold plates, bright sauces, citrus and herbs.
  • Warm Gentle heat for chowders, pots and the grill.
