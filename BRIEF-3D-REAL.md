# Showcase brief, round 3 — 5 REALISTIC 3D restaurant websites (one is Indo-Nepali)

Read in this order: `BRIEF.md`, `BRIEF-3D.md` (every rule there still applies: whole page is one 3D scene with the camera flown by scroll, lazy import, WebGL probe, poster fallback, pause button, reduced motion, skip link focus, contrast, no invented facts, footer line), then THIS file, which changes the look target from "stylised" to "realistic and aesthetic". Sites live in `showcase/sites/<slug>/`. Test URL: `http://localhost:8800/sites/<slug>/` (the shared server) or your own port.

## What "realistic" means here, and what it does not
Target: looks like a high-end product film or architectural visualisation still, not a game prop. Achieved by lighting, materials and real assets, not by polygon count.
- Real CC0 assets from Poly Haven (models, HDRI lighting maps, PBR textures). Fetch them with the helper: `python showcase/tools/fetch_polyhaven.py model <id> [1k|2k]`, `... hdri <id> [1k]`, `... texture <id> [1k|2k]`. They land in `showcase/assets/polyhaven/{models,hdri,textures}/<id>/` and are logged in `CREDITS.md` automatically. All CC0: free for commercial use, no attribution needed. Browse ids with `https://api.polyhaven.com/assets?t=models` (also `t=hdris`, `t=textures`; add `&c=<category>`). A 1k model is about 1-3 MB. Use 1k by default, 2k only for a hero object that fills the screen.
- Do NOT download from anywhere else (no Sketchfab, no stock photos, no AI images): licences and provenance matter for client work.
- Loaders you have: `vendor/three-addons/loaders/GLTFLoader.js`, `RGBELoader.js`, `FontLoader.js`; `vendor/three-addons/postprocessing/*` (EffectComposer, RenderPass, UnrealBloomPass, OutputPass, ShaderPass, BokehPass, SSAOPass, GTAOPass, FilmPass...), `lights/RectAreaLightUniformsLib.js`, `environments/RoomEnvironment.js`, `utils/BufferGeometryUtils.js`, three r160. No Draco/Meshopt decoders, so use plain glTF.
- Honest limit on food: Poly Haven has real fruit, vegetables, bread, cakes and a croissant, but NO cooked dishes (no curry, momos, steak, sushi). Do not fake photoreal food. Strategy: let real props, real materials and lighting carry the realism (brass, wood, ceramic, linen, fire, steam, produce), and show dishes as modest, well-lit, well-textured forms made from real produce models plus carefully built procedural pieces (pleated dumplings from lathe + displacement/normal detail, rice, sauces with a glossy PBR surface), clearly labelled as an "illustrative sample menu" in the HTML. Never put a flat cartoon dish in a realistic scene: if you cannot make a dish look right, frame it so it is partly hidden (steam, depth of field, a lid, a shadowed corner) or leave it out and let the vessel carry the shot.
- Do not claim realism you did not achieve. In your report say which things look realistic and which look procedural.

## Realism recipe (do all of it)
- Renderer: `outputColorSpace = SRGBColorSpace`, `toneMapping = ACESFilmicToneMapping` (exposure tuned per station), `useLegacyLights` is off by default in r160, so use physical light units sensibly.
- Lighting: an HDRI through `PMREMGenerator` as `scene.environment` (and a blurred or dark `scene.background` if the HDRI does not suit the story), plus 1-2 key lights (SpotLight or DirectionalLight) with soft shadows (`PCFSoftShadowMap`, shadow map 2048 on high, 1024 on mid, none on low, tune `bias` and `normalBias` so there is no acne or peter-panning), a warm practical per light source (candle, lantern, fire: PointLight with decay) and a rim light. Contact shadows or a blurred fake shadow plane under objects that the real shadows miss.
- Materials: use the textures that come with the glTFs. For surfaces you build yourself use Poly Haven PBR textures (`diff` as `SRGBColorSpace`, `nor` and `arm` as linear; ARM = AO in R, roughness in G, metalness in B: map with `aoMap`, `roughnessMap`, `metalnessMap`). Set `anisotropy` to the renderer max (cap at 8), repeat and wrap correctly, `MeshPhysicalMaterial` with clearcoat only where it reads (lacquer, glaze), never transmission on phones.
- Post (desktop high tier only): subtle bloom on emissive and highlights only (threshold high), optional GTAO for contact grounding, a very gentle vignette and film grain. Depth of field only if it holds 60 fps; fog or a darkening falloff is the cheap substitute. Keep FXAA and the shader fix from round 2 (drop the `-100` bias with `.replace(/,\s*-100\.0\)/g, ')')`).
- Camera: realistic lens (FOV 25-40 for hero shots), slow deliberate moves, no whip pans, no shaking. Frame like a photographer: foreground element, subject, background falloff. Damped lerp toward the scroll target.
- Composition over quantity: fewer, better-lit, better-placed objects beat a crowded scene. Real-world scale and believable placement (things rest on surfaces, nothing floats unless it is meant to).

## Load budget and loading experience (new, because real assets are heavy)
- Page HTML, CSS and text must be usable immediately. The 3D layer streams in behind a designed loading state (a progress line or a quiet branded mark; never a spinner on a blank page). Content is readable and scrollable while it loads.
- Load by station: the first station's assets first (the hero must appear within about 3 s on the dev GPU over localhost), later stations load in idle time or when the camera approaches. Missing or failed model: skip it, keep the scene, no console error beyond the failed request being handled; the poster fallback remains available.
- Budget: at most about 12 MB of 3D assets per site on the high tier, about 5 MB on the low (phone) tier (use smaller textures: pick `1k` and, on phones, downscale textures at load with a canvas or skip normal maps). Report the real numbers.
- Parse and upload without freezing: yield between model loads, upload textures one or two at a time, `renderer.compile` or `compileAsync` for the render target you actually use (round 2 Forno lost 2 s to shader compile in the first bloom frame). No main-thread block over about 250 ms.

## Bugs found in rounds 1 and 2: do not repeat
- rAF loop: store the id; re-queue only if `!raf`; `degrade()` idempotent; `wake()` safe; pause = zero rAF callbacks (also destroy or disable the Lenis, GSAP ticker and ScrollTrigger background loops; measure rAF per second while paused). Off-screen pause must watch the real content container, not the fixed full-screen canvas.
- Context loss: `preventDefault` on `webglcontextlost`, show the poster, on restore rebuild the environment map, composer and any render targets; do NOT dispose old-context objects after the restore (forget them), or Chrome logs dozens of errors; remove the `gl-lost` class before resizing.
- Page height must not change when the scene arrives or fails: an inline `<head>` probe sets `gl-able` and `rm`, layout keys on that from first paint, the probe releases its context.
- Remove all debug hooks before reporting done: no `window.__*`, no `?debug`/`?q=` query switches, no `console.log`.
- Text over the scene sits on a real panel (dark or light at 85-92% opacity with blur), never on a fading gradient; measure contrast against the real rendered pixels at every station at 1440, 1024 and 390 and fix every miss (>= 4.5:1 body, 3:1 large). No text that starts at low opacity.
- Focus rings must survive every button style: the later `box-shadow` of a button variant must not remove the dark halo; clip-path buttons need an alternative ring; ring contrast >= 3:1 against both sides.
- Pause button: constant label, `aria-pressed` carries the state, accessible name equals visible text (no aria-label that differs), hidden under reduced motion, also stops pointer parallax and magnetic effects, icon-only on phones with the text kept for screen readers.
- Mobile menu: `main` and footer `inert` while open, cleared on every exit path (Escape, link click, resize past the breakpoint), focus returns to the toggle, a menu link focuses its target section.
- Copy: no commercial claims or "of the day"/"fresh daily"/"best"/"free"/"authentic since" wording; dishes are generic category names flagged as an illustrative sample menu; prices `[Price]`; all contact, hours and address are visible `[placeholders]`. No cultural claims you cannot support (keep it to neutral descriptions of food and atmosphere). Fonts: system fonts, or open fonts saved locally with their licence file in `assets/` (see `sites/maison-claire/assets/CREDITS.txt` for the pattern).
- Phones: panels must not cover the whole scene at every station (round 2 complaint): short, compact panels at 390 wide and a visible 3D area between and around them.

## Verify before reporting (headless only: the user's screen must never show a browser window)
Playwright headless (`headless=True`; for a real GPU add `--use-angle=d3d11 --enable-gpu`; never headed, never `start`/`Start-Process`/`webbrowser.open`); close each browser after each test; other agents share the GPU so measure fps three times and report the median. Check at 1440, 1024, 390 (+720x450, 390x667): zero console errors, no horizontal scroll, WebGL off (poster fallback, page height stable), reduced motion, paused motion (identical frames, zero idle rAF), context loss and restore, skip link and menu focus, contrast sampled against rendered pixels at every station, time to first scene frame and total 3D bytes transferred. Look at a screenshot at every station and iterate on lighting and composition until it genuinely looks aesthetic; say honestly what still looks procedural. Do not mark your own work as tested.
